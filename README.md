## 🚀 What I'm Building

### EdoBiz Copilot

An AI-powered business assistant designed to help small businesses in Nigeria manage their business more easily using natural language.

EdoBiz Copilot aims to help business owners:

- Record sales and expenses
- Understand revenue and profitability
- Monitor cash flow
- Track business performance
- Get AI-powered business insights
- Interact using Nigerian English and Pidgin
- Build better digital financial records

**Status:** Prototype / In Development
